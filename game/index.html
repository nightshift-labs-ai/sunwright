HOW TO PLAY

1. BUILD

Spend quartz on glass. 1 Mirror bounces light, 2 Splitter halves it both ways, 3 Lens focuses x2.5 for 3 cells, 4 Pillar blocks beetles, 5 Prism (day 2) fans red burn, blue slow, gold pierce. Click to place, Q/E or wheel to turn, right-click or X to sell. You can never wall off the Sun Fountain.

2. BURN

Press ENTER. Sunlight pours through the lit window and follows your glass. Beetles on lit cells burn. Turn glass live to chase the swarm. SPACE fires a Flare: double power for 3 s. Quick kills build a SEAR multiplier.

3. ADAPT

After each sun pick 1 of 3 glass charms. The sun moves every wave - morning east, noon north, evening west - so plan for the NEXT window on the sun dial. Shellbacks shrug off weak light: focus it. Glassback shells stop every beam but gold.
