SUNWRIGHT

Mirrors of the Noon Courtyard

Guard the Sunseed shrine for 3 days - 9 waves of beetles

1-4 place glass - Q/E or wheel turn - SPACE Flare - ENTER start wave

The sun moves every wave: dawn east, noon north, dusk west

HOW TO PLAY

1. BUILD

Spend shards on glass. 1 Mirror bounces light, 2 Splitter halves it both ways, 3 Lens focuses x2.5 for 3 cells, 4 Pillar blocks beetles. Click to place, Q/E or wheel to turn, right-click to lift. You can never wall off the shrine.

2. BURN

Press ENTER. Sunlight pours through the lit window and follows your glass. Beetles on lit cells burn. Turn glass live to chase the swarm. SPACE fires a Flare: double power for 3 s. Quick kills build a CHAIN multiplier.

3. ADAPT

After each wave pick 1 of 3 boons. The sun moves every wave - dawn east, noon north, dusk west - so plan for the NEXT window on the sun dial. Shellbacks shrug off weak light: focus it.
